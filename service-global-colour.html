Hair Coloring

Global Colour & Gloss

Rich, even colour with a mirror-like gloss — from deep espresso to warm caramel.

What to expect

A single, considered shade applied with precision and sealed with a high-shine gloss. Ideal for refreshing tone, covering grey or deepening your natural colour.

Designed around you

Every MIRELLE service begins with a private consultation, so the products, techniques and timing are chosen for you — not from a template.

How the service works

1. Colour consultation

We begin by understanding your hair, skin and the way you like to feel.

2. Custom formulation

Everything is prepared and tailored to your needs before we start.

3. Application & processing

The core of your service is delivered with calm, unhurried attention.

4. Gloss, wash & finish

A polished finish and clear aftercare so your result lasts.

  • Root-to-end colour
  • High-shine gloss
  • Blow-dry finish
