Five Habits for Healthier-Looking Hair

MWritten byMIRELLE StudioUpdatedSep 30, 2026

Healthy hair is built on habits far more than on any single product. You do not need a twelve-step routine; you need a handful of consistent behaviours that protect the hair you have and let it grow strong. These are the five we recommend to almost every client.

1. Mind the water temperature

Very hot water roughens the surface of the hair and can leave the scalp irritated and dry. Wash with lukewarm water and finish with a cooler rinse to help the surface lie flat and reflect more light.

Rinse thoroughly. Product left behind weighs hair down and dulls colour, so spend longer rinsing than you think you need.

2. Protect from heat, every time

Use a heat protectant before blow-drying, curling or straightening, and keep tools at the lowest temperature that gets the job done. Fine or lightened hair rarely needs more than a moderate setting.

Whenever you can, let hair air-dry most of the way before you style. Less time under heat means less cumulative damage.

  • Apply protectant to damp, towel-dried hair
  • Avoid going over the same section repeatedly
  • Let tools cool before storing them

3. Condition with intention

Conditioner belongs on the lengths and ends, where hair is oldest and driest, not on the scalp. Leave it on for a minute or two rather than rinsing straight away.

Add a deeper mask once a week, or after colour and heat styling. Choose a formula for your needs: moisture for dryness, protein-rich care for stretch and breakage.

4. Be gentle when hair is wet

Wet hair is at its most fragile. Detangle from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb or a flexible brush, and blot with a soft towel instead of rubbing.

Sleep matters too. A silk or satin pillowcase, or a loose braid, reduces friction overnight and keeps styles fresher for longer.

5. Book regular trims

Removing split ends every eight to twelve weeks stops damage travelling up the strand. Trimming does not make hair grow faster, but it keeps the length you grow looking thick and healthy.

If you are growing hair out, a small dusting rather than a full cut preserves length while keeping the ends in good condition.
