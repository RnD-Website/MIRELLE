FAQ

Explore all essential questions answered.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking one to two weeks ahead, and four to six weeks for colour, bridal or weekend appointments. Walk-ins are welcome when there is space.

Are your studio and tools hygienic?

Yes. Every station, tool and basin is sanitised between clients, and our team follows strict hygiene and safety protocols.

Which products do you use? Are they safe?

We work with carefully selected professional haircare and skincare brands chosen for performance and ingredient quality. Tell us about any allergies before your service.

Do you offer consultations?

Yes. Every new client receives a complimentary consultation before any service begins, so the result is planned around you.

Can I choose my stylist?

Of course. You can request a stylist when you book, and we will do our best to match your preferred day and time.

How long do services take?

A cut takes around an hour, colour and treatments two to three hours, and bridal services are planned with you in advance. Each service page shows its duration.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment so we can offer the time to another client.

Do you offer bridal and event styling?

Yes. We offer bridal hair and makeup with a full trial, as well as styling for events, on-site or in the studio.

Do you sell gift cards?

Gift cards are available in the studio. Ask our front desk and we will help you choose a service or an amount.

How should I prepare for my first visit?

Arrive with clean, dry hair if possible, bring photos of looks you like, and let us know your hair history. We will take care of the rest.
