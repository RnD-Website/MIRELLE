Our story

Hear our story

MIRELLE began as a simple idea: a studio where the consultation matters as much as the cut. We wanted a space that felt more like a private lounge than a busy salon — soft light, warm hospitality and honest advice.

Too many beauty visits felt rushed and templated. So we slowed down, listened first, and built a team where hair and skin are looked after as part of one thoughtful ritual.

Today our stylists, colourists and skin therapists work together under one roof, creating looks that are polished, wearable and entirely yours.

Our values

The things that truly matter.

Expertise

Continuous training and a high bar for craft in every chair.

Personalisation

Nothing is templated — every look is designed around your texture, tone and lifestyle.

Calm

An unhurried studio where you can switch off and enjoy the ritual.
